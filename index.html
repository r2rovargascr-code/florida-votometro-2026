Sources, methods and photo credits

Candidate positions come from candidates' own websites, survey answers and statements, their recorded votes, and news reporting; every position links to its source in your results. When a candidate has no documented position on a question, that question is left out of their score instead of being guessed. Positions marked "implied" are based on closely related statements or votes rather than a direct answer to the question.

Photos: official U.S. Congress portraits (Donalds, Jolly, Moody; public domain); Wikimedia Commons (Nixon, Dimanche); the candidates' own campaign websites (Abrams, Burkett, Gillespie, Jewett, Russo); and official Florida Senate and Florida House member photos for current legislators. Candidates shown with initials had no suitable photo available.

District races: candidate lists from the Florida Division of Elections; legislators' positions from official roll-call votes published by the Florida Senate.

Inspired by Costa Rica's Votómetro. Check your official sample ballot with your county Supervisor of Elections.
